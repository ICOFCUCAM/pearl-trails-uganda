How permits work

  • Named and dated. A permit carries your passport name and one date. It cannot be moved, resold or refunded once issued.
  • Paid in full at reservation. UWA does not hold permits against a deposit, so the permit portion of your invoice is due immediately.
  • Released two years ahead. June to September and December sell out first, often four to six months before travel.
  • Sector allocated on the day. The park allocates families at the 07:00 briefing. Tell the warden if you want a short walk.

04

Deposits, balances and what happens if plans change.

Published in full because the permit rules are unusual and nobody should meet them for the first time on an invoice.

Deposit

Thirty per cent of the land price secures the vehicle, guide and lodges. The permit portion is payable in full at the same moment because UWA issues, rather than reserves, on payment.

Balance

Due sixty days before arrival. Bookings made inside sixty days are payable in full at confirmation. We invoice in US dollars and accept transfer, card with a 3.2% fee, or mobile money in country.

Cancellation

More than sixty days: deposit retained, permits non-refundable. Thirty to sixty days: fifty per cent. Inside thirty days: full price. This mirrors what the lodges charge us.

Postponement

We will move a booking once at no fee if you tell us more than sixty days out and the new dates are within twelve months. Permits cannot move; a new permit must be bought.

If a trek fails

UWA refunds part of a gorilla permit if the family is not found, which is rare. We hand the refund on in full and add the day back to your itinerary where the routing allows.

Price validity

Quotes hold for fourteen days. Once the deposit is paid the land price is fixed, including against fuel movement. Government fee changes are passed through at cost, up or down.
