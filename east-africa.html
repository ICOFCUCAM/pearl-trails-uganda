Fourteen countries, one region.

Uganda is where we run our own vehicles and guides. The rest we arrange with operators we have worked beside for years — and every one of them gets the same twenty-seven tourism experiences here.

Uganda The Pearl of Africa

Bwindi and Mgahinga for gorillas, Kibale for chimpanzees, Queen Elizabeth and Murchison for the savannah, Jinja for the Nile and the Rwenzori for altitude.
Kenya Into the Great Savannah

Maasai Mara and Amboseli for the plains game, Mount Kenya for altitude, Lamu and Diani for the coast, Nairobi for the only national park inside a capital city.
Tanzania Under the Shadow of Kilimanjaro

Serengeti and Ngorongoro for the plains, Kilimanjaro and Meru for altitude, Zanzibar and Mafia for the reef, and the Swahili coast between them.
Rwanda Land of a Thousand Hills

Volcanoes National Park for gorillas, Nyungwe for canopy and chimpanzees, Lake Kivu for the shoreline and Kigali for the arrival.
Burundi Drums Above Tanganyika

Lake Tanganyika for the shoreline, Bujumbura for the arrival, the Gitega hills for the drummers and Kibira for the forest.
01Where the Nile Spreads OutPhotograph pending · Hero Destination
South Sudan The Nile and the Great Wetland

The White Nile at Juba, the Sudd wetland, the Boma–Badingilo migration and the cattle camps of the Nile floodplain.
Ethiopia The Roof of Africa

Lalibela and the northern circuit, the Simien and Bale mountains, the Danakil Depression, the Omo valley and Addis Ababa.
Somalia The Longest Coast in Africa

The Mogadishu and Berbera coast, the Laas Geel rock art, the Golis escarpment and the frankincense country of the northeast.
Djibouti Salt, Rift and Whale Shark

Lake Assal and Lake Abbé for the volcanic ground, the Gulf of Tadjoura for the whale sharks, the Day forest for the last of the green.
01A Modernist City at AltitudePhotograph pending · Hero Destination
Eritrea Asmara, and the Red Sea Below

Asmara for the architecture, Massawa for the coral port, the Dahlak islands for the reef and the Filfil escarpment for the green.
01The Perfumed IslandsPhotograph pending · Hero Destination
Comoros The Perfumed Islands

Grande Comore for Karthala and Moroni, Mohéli for the turtles and whales, Anjouan for the waterfalls and the perfume distilleries.
Seychelles Granite, Palm and Turquoise

Mahé for arrival and mountain, Praslin for the Vallée de Mai, La Digue for Anse Source d'Argent, and the outer atolls for the rest.
01The Lagoon and the MountainPhotograph pending · Hero Destination
Mauritius Lagoon, Mountain and Melting Pot

The lagoon and reef for the water, Black River Gorges and Le Morne for the interior, Port Louis and Grand Baie for the rest.
01An Island That Evolved AlonePhotograph pending · Hero Destination
Madagascar An Island That Evolved Alone

Andasibe and Ranomafana for the lemurs, the Avenue of the Baobabs and Tsingy for the west, Nosy Be and Île Sainte-Marie for the coast.
