The trackers stop, and then nobody speaks.

You have been climbing since half past seven through nettles and wet vine, and the radio has been saying the same thing for an hour. Then it says something different, the machetes go quiet, and eight metres of undergrowth away a silverback carries on eating as though the whole morning had been arranged for him.

Between the parks · what a driving day is

The road is half the trip, so we are honest about it.

Ishasha to Kibale is 168 kilometres and takes five hours, because the last forty are murram and the last ten are a market. We publish the distances before you book and we do not schedule a game drive at the end of a transfer day.

02

Where we actually go, and why each one.

Ten national parks, four of them within a day of each other. These are the ones that end up on almost every itinerary we write.

Bwindi Impenetrable 1,160–2,607 m · four sectors

Half the world's mountain gorillas. Ruhija for the shortest drives, Rushaga for the habituation experience, Buhoma for the easiest walking.

Kibale Forest 795 km² · 13 primate species

The best chimpanzee tracking in East Africa, plus red colobus, L'Hoest's monkey and a wetland walk at Bigodi that birders refuse to hurry.

Queen Elizabeth Kazinga Channel · Ishasha

A two-hour boat between two lakes with more hippo than water, and the fig trees in the south where the lions climb.

Murchison Falls 3,893 km² · the Nile gorge

The largest park in Uganda, split by the river. Buligi for the plains game at dawn, the launch upstream for the full weight of the falls.

Uganda has no bad month, only different mud.

On the equator the rain is a matter of hours, not seasons. What changes is the state of the forest track, the price of a bed and how far ahead the permits go.

Dec – Feb

Short dry

Firm tracks, clear mornings on the crater lakes, and the easiest trekking of the year. Lodges at peak rate and permits gone early.

Book 6 months out
Mar – May

Long rains

Green, empty and cheap. Afternoon downpours, heavy going in Bwindi, and the best month of the year for birds on migration.

Low-season rates
Jun – Sep

Long dry

Our busiest window. Best road conditions, best chance of a short trek, and the only period when permits routinely sell out.

Book 4–6 months out
Oct – Nov

Short rains

Shoulder season and quietly the best value. Rain in short bursts, forests at their loudest, and lodges willing to talk about rates.
